**Jane’s Walk** is a global movement of free, citizen-led walking conversations inspired by the ideas of Jane Jacobs. The initiative invites people to share stories about their neighbourhoods, discover overlooked aspects of their communities, and connect with one another through the simple act of walking. ([janeswalk.org](https://janeswalk.org))

Led by Justyna Borucka, a group of students from Politechnika Gdańska developed and conducted a themed city walk titled *Running Waters*. Following a carefully planned route along the river as it emerged and disappeared throughout the urban fabric, the walk explored the often-hidden relationship between water and the city.

The walk concluded at a designated site, where participants were welcomed by a large-scale installation offering an abstract interpretation of the city’s waters. The site also served as a gathering space, hosting a small cocktail reception and a series of engaging events that brought the experience to a lively close.
