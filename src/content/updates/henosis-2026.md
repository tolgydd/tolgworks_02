Presented at the 3rd Adana Architecture Festival at Adana Museum Complex, 15–17 October 2026, HENOSIS is an interactive spatial installation exploring collective sensing through real-time brain activity. Bringing together architecture, neuroscience and generative audiovisual systems, it asks how space can emerge through the encounter between two minds.

Two participants enter the installation simultaneously. Their EEG data is processed in real time, shaping the movement, colour and sound of a shared atmosphere. Each participant retains a distinct presence while contributing to a composition that continually changes through their interaction.

[[image 2]]

This relationship works in both directions: brain activity transforms the atmosphere, while its evolving images and sounds influence perception and attention. HENOSIS approaches collective mind as a possibility arising through difference and exchange, inviting participants to reconsider where individual experience ends and a shared experience begins.

[[image 3]]

Perhaps sensing together is not feeling the same thing, but becoming part of one another’s becoming.
